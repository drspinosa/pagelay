This policy explains how the PageLay name, logo and associated branding may be used. It sits alongside the [MIT License](LICENSE), which covers the PageLay source code.

- **Product:** PageLay
- **Creator and developer:** Adam Spinos
- **Publisher:** Look4ideas Designs LLP (Companies House number OC354341)

Look4ideas Designs LLP is the business intended to manage the PageLay branding.

## In short

- The PageLay **source code** is open source under the MIT License. You can use, modify and sell it, including in commercial forks under your own name and branding.
- The PageLay **name, logo and visual identity** are reserved. You can share PageLay and talk about it freely, but commercial redistribution under the original PageLay branding needs permission.
- Truthful references and compatibility statements are always welcome.

## Status of this policy

- PageLay™ is used as a trade mark for the PageLay software. This policy does not claim that PageLay or its logo is a registered trade mark.
- This policy states how we intend the branding to be used. It does not by itself create registered trade mark rights, and it applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law.
- Nothing in this policy limits the rights the MIT License grants for the source code, or any use the law allows without permission, such as truthful, factual references to PageLay.

## What this policy covers

"PageLay branding" means:

- the name **PageLay**, when used to identify software, a product or a service;
- the PageLay logo, including the "PL" mark;
- the following branding asset files in this repository:
  - `public/favicon.svg`
  - `public/favicon.ico`
  - `public/apple-touch-icon.png`
- any other logos, icons or visual identity published as PageLay branding in future.

## Relationship with the MIT License

The MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) is intended to apply to the PageLay source code. It is not intended to grant rights to the PageLay branding, and the branding asset files listed above are excluded from the MIT grant. The MIT License does not include a trade mark licence.

## Uses that do not need permission

You may, without asking:

- use PageLay for any purpose, including at work or in a business;
- refer to PageLay by name in articles, reviews, tutorials, documentation, teaching, talks and social media;
- link to the official PageLay repository or website;
- share copies of the official PageLay release, with its branding intact, on a non-commercial basis, for example with friends, colleagues, students or a community, or through a free mirror, provided you do not suggest that you are the publisher;
- state truthfully that your software is "based on PageLay", "a fork of PageLay" or "derived from PageLay source code";
- state truthfully that your software can open, import, read, write or export `.lay` files, or works with PageLay documents.

Please write the name as "PageLay" and avoid wording that suggests your software is official or endorsed when it is not.

## Forks, including commercial forks

You are welcome to fork and modify PageLay under the MIT License, and to use, distribute or sell your fork commercially. If you distribute or host a fork publicly, please:

1. give it your own name, not "PageLay" or anything confusingly similar;
2. replace the PageLay logo and the branding asset files listed above with your own;
3. make clear that it is not the official PageLay and is not endorsed by Adam Spinos or Look4ideas Designs LLP;
4. keep the MIT copyright and licence notice, as the MIT License requires.

Factual statements such as "based on PageLay" remain welcome.

## Uses that need permission

Please ask first if you want to:

- redistribute PageLay commercially under its original branding, for example by selling it, charging for access or downloads, bundling it in a paid product or service, or listing it in an app store under the PageLay name;
- use "PageLay", or a confusingly similar name, as the name of your own product, service, company, app or domain;
- use the PageLay logo or the branding asset files in your own software, website, marketing or merchandise;
- describe your software or service as official, certified, approved or endorsed by PageLay.

Permission must be given in writing by Look4ideas Designs LLP. There is no commercial branding licence programme at present; requests will be considered individually, and any future commercial arrangement would be agreed separately.

## Your documents and the `.lay` format

This policy does not affect documents you create with PageLay. You keep whatever rights you have in your documents, including `.lay` files and exported files, and you may share and use them, including commercially.

This policy does not restrict anyone from creating, opening or exchanging `.lay` files, or from writing software that reads or writes them. It concerns only the use of the PageLay name and branding.
